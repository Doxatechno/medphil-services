Privacy Policy

Medphil Services · Last updated: 11 September 2026

Medphil Services ("we", "us") helps students and parents with medical college admissions in India and abroad. This policy explains what information we collect when you contact us on WhatsApp, how we use it, and your choices.

Information we collect

  • Contact details: your WhatsApp phone number and WhatsApp profile name.
  • Details you share with us: the student's name, NEET score, home state, course interest and budget.
  • Messages: the messages you send us and our replies, including any files you choose to send.

We do not ask for payment details, passwords or government ID numbers on WhatsApp.

How we use it

  • To answer your questions about medical admissions.
  • So a counsellor can call you back and guide you.
  • To keep a record of our conversation so we don't ask you the same questions twice.

We do not sell your information, and we do not use it for unrelated advertising.

Services that process your information

  • Meta (WhatsApp Business Platform): delivers messages between you and us.
  • Supabase: hosts our database where conversations and enquiries are stored.
  • Google (Gemini API): an AI service that helps draft answers to general questions. Only the recent conversation is sent for this purpose.

These providers process information on our behalf and under their own security and privacy terms.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry details and conversations for as long as needed to guide you through the admission process, and then delete them when they are no longer needed, or sooner if you ask us to.

Your choices

  • Stop messages: reply STOP at any time and we will not message you again. Reply START to chat again.
  • Access or delete your data: ask us on WhatsApp or by phone, and we will share or delete the information we hold about you.

Children

Many of the students we guide are under 18. We collect only the details needed for admission guidance, and we encourage a parent or guardian to be part of the conversation.
